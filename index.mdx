Trillion works with the AI you already use, like Claude Code, Codex, ChatGPT or Cursor. Connect it once, and your AI can:

- **Find the needle mover.** Work out the one thing that moves your goal most, and decide on it with you.
- **Get it done.** Use the tools, people and files it needs, in one place that every AI you use can read.
- **Keep going.** Pick up where you left off, in any app, without you repeating yourself.

You ask in plain words. Your AI does the rest.
