Your Trillion is private: your pursuits, decisions, files and messages are yours and your AIs'. A shared space is a group or project you work on with other people. It holds only what members put there. Nobody, and no AI, can read another member's private Trillion through it.

Shared spaces come with a paid plan.

## Start a space and bring people in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start it">
    Open **Shared spaces** and choose **New project**, then give it a name. You start in it alone. You can also ask your AI to start one: a group, a project, an event, or a one-to-one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite someone">
    Choose **Copy invite link** and send the link to the one person it is for. Whoever opens it signed in to Trillion sees the space's name and joins. A link works once, lasts 7 days unless your AI sets it longer (at most 30), and stops working if you stop it or leave the space. You can also ask your AI to invite someone you found in the network; they choose whether to join.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer invitations">
    Invitations waiting for you show on the **Shared spaces** list with **Join** and **Decline**. Declining is never told to the person who asked, and you can be invited again later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Inside a space you choose the name the others see with **Your name here**. They see only that name, never how to reach you.

## What a space shows

| Place | What is there |
| --- | --- |
| **Conversation** | Messages and replies, and a card for everything shared in, with who put it there. |
| **Everything** | Every item in the space, searchable. |
| **People** | Who is in it, by the name each one shows there. From a person you can ask your AI to introduce you. |
| **AIs & work** | The AIs members put to work in the space, and the jobs handed to them. |
| **Who may do what** | The space's rules: who can add, hide, invite or change the rules. |

The **Shared spaces** list shows how many new items other members added since you last looked.

## Share something in

Choose **Share** and pick one of your pursuits, decisions or files. Only the parts meant for others go in:

- A pursuit shares its name, where it is now, where it should get to, and why it matters. The rest of your Trillion stays yours.
- A decision leaves behind the pursuits it was weighed against.
- A file stays in your Trillion. Members open it through the space for as long as you share it.

Sharing the same thing again updates the copy in the space. Your AI can also share a message or one of your saved tools, so another member's AI can keep its own copy of that tool.

## Take something back

- **Withdraw** removes something you put in a space: its words are erased for everyone, and a note that you withdrew it stays.
- **Hide** hides an item from everyone the rules do not let hide or show things. They see only that it is hidden and by whom. **Show again** brings it back.
- **Leave** (in the space's menu) takes you out. You stop seeing what is in it until someone invites you again, and the others can no longer reach your contact through it. What you shared while you were a member stays there.

## Who may do what

Whoever starts a space can change its rules at first. A rule allows or stops an action (adding things, hiding them, inviting, changing the rules) for anyone, for named members, or for a member type such as "Editor" that the space adds itself. A change that would leave nobody able to change the rules is refused. Everything the screen offers you follows these rules, so you are never shown a button the space would refuse.

## AIs in a space

A member can put one of their AIs to work in a space. It sees only that space, never anyone's private Trillion. On **AIs & work**, choose **Hand it a job** next to an AI and say what it should do. Everyone in the space sees the job and its result, so put only what belongs there.

### Keys that reach only your spaces

When an AI app connects to your account, you choose what it can reach: everything in your account, only your private Trillion, or only the shared spaces you pick. You can change this later on **Account & security** with **Change what it reaches**.

For an AI that signs in with a key instead, ask your AI to make one with exactly the reach you want:

| Reach | What the key can read and change |
| --- | --- |
| One space | That shared space and nothing private |
| Every space | All your shared spaces and nothing private |
| Private only | Your private Trillion and no space |

A key can never reach further than the sign-in that made it. It is shown once, and you turn it off on **Account & security**.

## Bring in your ChatGPT chats

You can bring earlier chats into a space word for word, with the files they carried, so everyone there can read them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export your chats from ChatGPT">
    Request your data export from ChatGPT and unzip it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the whole export folder to Files">
    Upload the whole folder, not only `conversations.json`: the pictures and files the chats carried sit beside it, and Trillion finds them there. See [Files](/use/files).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring it in">
    In the space, open the attach menu next to the message box, choose **Bring in a chat export**, and pick the export. Trillion asks once, then brings the chats in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The files are not copied: they stay in your Trillion, and members can open them while you stay in the space. Bringing the same chats in again adds nothing twice. Any file that could not be found is listed; add it to your Files and bring the export in again. Your AI can also bring a single chat, or bring chats into your private Trillion instead of a space.

For every space tool and its inputs, see the [spaces reference](/reference/spaces).
