A pursuit is something you are working toward, written as where it stands today and where you want it to get. Every AI you connect reads your pursuits, and every [decision](/use/decisions) is weighed against them. The more specific they are, the better the decisions: a vague pursuit makes vague decisions.

## What a pursuit holds

| Part | Example |
| --- | --- |
| What it is | Win the club final |
| Where it is now | Lost in the quarter-finals last year |
| Where you want it | Champion in June |
| Why it matters | In your words |
| What shows it | Where each fact came from, with its date |

Your AI fills your pursuits from what it already knows about you (its memory of you, your files, your past conversations) before asking you anything, and cites where it found each one. You can also tell it directly: "I'm working toward opening a second studio by spring."

When a pursuit ends it is marked ended, never erased.

## Your biggest needle mover

One pursuit can be marked as your **biggest needle mover**: the one that, done, moves the others most. It shows first on your Home, and it is where your AIs put their effort. At the start of a conversation, after any work waiting for them, your AIs read your pursuits and act on the biggest needle mover.

Choosing the move follows one rule: the most leverage, the move that changes your situation the most for the least time, energy, money and complexity. Your AI starts with a decision, then uses what is already kept in your Trillion, then your other data, and reaches for tools and people last.

<Tip>
In the iPhone app, Home opens on your biggest needle mover. Tap the needle button on another pursuit to make it the one, or choose **Have my AI pick** when none is marked yet.
</Tip>

## Progress

**Progress** shows what is waiting for your answer, what you are working toward, and what helped.

- **What you are working toward** lists your pursuits.
- **Over time** plots your decisions, oldest on the left: how many you made, how long each took to decide, and how many of those with a result reached their goal. A decision counts toward goals hit once you say how it went.
- **Share your progress** makes a card with your numbers only, never what your decisions were about. Share it to X, LinkedIn, Facebook, WhatsApp, a text message or email, copy its link, or save the picture. Whoever opens the link sees only the numbers.

### What helped

Ask your AI "what has helped me decide well?" It reads your own saved decisions: how many hit their goal, how long it took from deciding to a checked result, whether your latest decisions are faster or more often right than earlier ones, and which of the method's questions most often changed your answer. Every figure comes with its counts, and below a minimum it says there is not enough data yet.

## On the free plan

The free plan walks decisions through the method but keeps nothing: no decisions, notes or pursuits are stored. A paid plan keeps them, so each decision builds on the last and Progress has something to show.

For the pursuit tools and their inputs, see [setup and goals](/reference/setup-and-goals).
