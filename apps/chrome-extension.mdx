---
title: "Chrome extension"
description: "Send to Trillion sends any page, post, picture or selection from your browser to your AIs, with what you want done with it."
---

**Send to Trillion** is a browser extension for Chrome and other Chromium browsers (Arc, Dia, Edge, Brave). When you find something worth acting on, you send it to your Trillion with an instruction or a note, and an AI on your account picks it up and acts on it.

## What it sends

- **This page**: the page as it describes itself, with its picture, site, title, description and address.
- **Selected text**: whatever you highlighted.
- **Picture of the page**: what you see right now, kept in your [files](/use/files).
- **Anything you right-click**: on any page, right-click a post, picture, video, link or passage and choose **Add to Trillion**. A right-click on a post takes the post itself (its address, words and pictures), not only the page. It waits in the extension until you send.

## Send something

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Click **Send to Trillion** in the toolbar, or press **Option-Shift-S** (Alt-Shift-S on Windows).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where it goes">
    Under **Send to**, tick one or more places. **My Trillion** is only you and your AIs. Each [shared space](/use/spaces) you are in is everyone in that space.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what your AI should do">
    Pick an instruction under **What should your AI do?** and, if you like, **Add a note for your AI**. On some sites it starts on the instruction you chose for that site (Instagram starts on its own).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who takes it">
    Under **Who takes it**, leave **Whichever AI is free**, or pick one of your AIs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Press **Send**. When it says **Sent**, an AI on your account will pick it up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If one place receives the send and another does not, the extension says which places already have it and asks you to check the last one before sending again, so nothing is sent twice by accident.

## What happens to what you send

In **My Trillion**, it arrives as a job for your AIs with the instruction you picked, your note, and everything you attached; whichever AI takes it acts on it. In a shared space, each file is shared into the space and the links, words and note are added as one item that everyone there can see.

If you choose no instruction, the job reads: "Work out what this is, whether it is valuable or related to anything I am working on, and act on it if it is."

## Change the places and instructions

Choose **Manage** in the extension. It opens the **Sharing** screen in Trillion, where you:

- put the places in order, hide a space, and choose which are ticked at the start,
- add, rename or remove instructions,
- pick which instruction a site starts on.

The extension reads this list each time it opens, so a change shows up the next time you open it. The iPhone share sheet and the Android share page read the same list.

## Signing in

The first time you open it, the extension shows the page and one button, **Sign in to Trillion**. It opens Trillion's sign-in in a tab; sign in with your passkey. The tab says **You're signed in** and closes. From then on, open Send to Trillion from any page to send what you find. There is no password and no key to copy.

## What it can reach

The extension talks only to app.gettrillion.ai. It reads the page you are on only when you open it or right-click something, keeps its sign-in in the browser's own storage, and adds the **Add to Trillion** right-click item.

## Getting it

Send to Trillion is not listed on the Chrome Web Store yet, so there is no one-click install today. Until it is listed, use the [iPhone share sheet](/apps/iphone), the Android share menu, or ask your AI directly with the link you want it to act on.
