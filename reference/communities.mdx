A community is a shared space with its own rules for who may join, a feed of posts, events, courses, levels and rewards. A community never shares anyone's contact. Its owner can change how it works and let members be paired to meet each other.

You do not call these tools yourself. You ask your AI in plain words, and it picks the tool. This page shows what each one does, so you know what to ask for.

## Communities

`list_communities` · Reads only

Communities this person can see or is in: title, what it is about, how many members, whether they are a member and whether they may join (and what joining comes with).

It takes no inputs.

```json Example
{}
```

## Open a community

`community` · Reads only

One community, or the parts any space has turned on (pass its space id), as this person may see it: what it is, their level, points and the next level, rewards they can claim, events (with who is going, for members), the feed of posts and replies, and members with their levels. Spaces a community keeps for members are left out for anyone else. For the owner it also has claims to hand out and the configuration.

**Inputs**

| Input | Type | Required | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `community_id` | string | Yes | A community id from `list_communities` |
| `limit` | integer |  | How many posts of the feed |

```json Example
{
  "community_id": "<community_id>"
}
```
