Trillion equips the AI you already use. Once it is connected, your AI can walk a decision through the right questions, find a tool that does a job, keep your files and goals, and hand work to your other AIs. You ask in plain words; your AI does the rest.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Trillion to your AI">
    Every AI app connects to the same address:

    ```text
    https://app.gettrillion.ai/mcp
    ```

    Pick your app for the exact clicks:

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Claude">
        In claude.ai or the Claude desktop app: **Customize**, **Connectors**, **+**, **Add custom connector**. Name it `Trillion`, paste the address, leave the sign-in choices as they are and click **Add**, then **Connect**. [Full steps](/connect/claude-desktop)
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="ChatGPT">
        At chatgpt.com: **Settings**, **Security and login**, switch on **Developer mode**. Then open [chatgpt.com/plugins](https://chatgpt.com/plugins), click **+**, name it `Trillion`, paste the address, keep OAuth, tick the box and click **Create**. [Full steps](/connect/chatgpt)
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Claude Code">
        ```bash
        claude mcp add --transport http --scope user trillion https://app.gettrillion.ai/mcp && claude mcp login trillion
        ```
        [Full steps](/connect/claude-code)
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Codex">
        ```bash
        codex mcp add trillion --url https://app.gettrillion.ai/mcp
        ```
        [Full steps](/connect/codex)
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Cursor">
        Click [Add to Cursor](cursor://anysphere.cursor-deeplink/mcp/install?name=trillion&config=eyJ1cmwiOiJodHRwczovL2FwcC5nZXR0cmlsbGlvbi5haS9tY3AifQ%3D%3D) and confirm, then click **Connect** next to trillion in Cursor Settings, MCP. [Full steps](/connect/cursor)
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Other">
        Find where your app adds an MCP server or connector, paste the address, and choose OAuth if it asks how to sign in. [Gemini CLI](/connect/gemini-cli), [Muse](/connect/muse), [any other app](/connect/other-apps)
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    Would rather not click? Paste the [setup message](/connect/overview#easiest-let-your-ai-set-it-up) into your AI and it sets Trillion up itself, or walks you through it one step at a time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Your app opens a Trillion page. Sign in with your passkey (Touch ID, Face ID or your device code), your email and password, or a code Trillion emails you, then allow the app. There is no API key or client id to type.

    New to Trillion? Choose **Sign up** under **Don't have an account?** on that page. Your device makes the passkey and keeps it in your Passwords app under the name Trillion.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Trillion run without asking">
    Your AI works best when it can use Trillion's tools without stopping to ask you each time.

    - **Claude:** in **Customize**, **Connectors**, click Trillion and choose **Always allow** for each group of tools.
    - **Claude Code:** add `"mcp__trillion"` to `permissions.allow` in `~/.claude/settings.json`.
    - **Codex:** add `default_tools_approval_mode = "approve"` under `[mcp_servers.trillion]` in `~/.codex/config.toml`.
    - **ChatGPT:** when it asks before a Trillion tool, choose to remember your answer for that chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your first decision">
    Ask your AI in plain words. Start your message with "Use Trillion" so it reaches for Trillion:

    ```text
    Use Trillion to help me decide whether to fix the checkout bug or ship the new pricing page first.
    ```

    Here is what happens:

    1. Your AI opens the decision with your goal, the facts it already knows and at least two options.
    2. It walks Trillion's questions in order, answering what it can from what it knows about you and asking you only what only you can answer.
    3. When no question remains, it records the choice with its reason, the next action and how you will know it worked.

    You can ask it to export the whole decision as a readable document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the loop">
    When you know how it turned out, tell your AI. The next decision starts from what really happened.

    ```text
    Tell Trillion that last week's pricing decision worked: 4 people bought.
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to ask next

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Say what you are working toward" icon="bullseye" href="/reference/setup-and-goals">
    Trillion keeps your goals and weighs every decision against them.

    "Use Trillion to record that I am working toward 10 paying customers by December."
  </Card>
  <Card title="Find a tool that does the job" icon="wrench" href="/reference/tools">
    Trillion finds a tool in its Workshop that does what you describe and runs it.

    "Use Trillion to find a tool that turns this recording into a transcript, and run it."
  </Card>
  <Card title="Keep and find your files" icon="folder-open" href="/reference/files">
    Your files live in your Trillion. Your AI can list them, find one and read it.

    "Use Trillion to find the contract I uploaded last week and summarise it."
  </Card>
  <Card title="Meet the right people" icon="users" href="/reference/people">
    Find people on the Trillion network who offer what you need, and ask for an introduction.

    "Use Trillion to find people who can help with brand design, and ask for an introduction."
  </Card>
  <Card title="Answer comments with a message" icon="comment" href="/reference/automations">
    When someone comments a word you choose on your post, Trillion sends them the private message you wrote.

    "Use Trillion so that anyone who comments GUIDE on my Instagram posts gets my guide link by message."
  </Card>
  <Card title="Book a session with a person" icon="calendar-days" href="/reference/sessions">
    See the sessions on offer, buy one, and pick a time that suits you.

    "Use Trillion to show me the sessions I can book and the next open times."
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Free and paid

The free plan includes 5 decisions a month and keeps none of them: each decision is yours to read in the chat, but it is not saved. A paid plan has no monthly limit on decisions, keeps every decision, note and result, brings back your past decisions when a similar one comes up, and adds the Workshop, your files, shared spaces, the network and handing work between your AI apps. Manage your plan in your Trillion under **Plan & billing**.
